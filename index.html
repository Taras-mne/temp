What is Foldy

Everything a cloud drive gives your team. Except the cloud.

One folder on your drive becomes the team’s shared space. Teammates mount it with a short code; their apps and AI agents work in it like a local folder. Every change lands on your drive — and shows up for everyone, live.

How it works

Four steps.
Nothing leaves your drive.

01

Pickthe project folder

The repo, the deck, the raw footage — any folder on your drive. Foldy remembers where it lives. It doesn’t copy it.

02

Shareone short code

Paste it in Slack or read it out on a call. A teammate types it into Foldy — that’s the whole invite. Close the share and the code expires.

03

Mounton every machine

The folder shows up live on each teammate’s laptop — read, write, drag, delete. Files stream on demand over a direct, encrypted tunnel.

04

Workalongside your agents

Point Cursor, Claude Code or any local agent at the mounted folder. What it writes lands on the host’s drive, and the whole team sees it.

Every byte between devices is end-to-end encrypted over WebRTC, keyed by an Ed25519 identity that lives on the device. Keys never leave your machine, so Foldy can’t read your files even if we wanted to.
